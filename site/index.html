Octometer

Every click in your app, counted.

Octometer counts each click in your web app. One script tag, one dashboard, no cookie banner.

Join the waitlist

Octometer is in a pilot. Join the waitlist, and we invite you.

How it works

  1. Register your app

    You sign in with a mail link, you register your app, and you get a key.

  2. Add one script tag

    You paste one script tag into your pages. Nothing else changes in your app.

  3. Open your dashboard

    The dashboard shows the clicks, the users, and the elements of your app within a minute.

Three levels in your dashboard

The dashboard opens on the apps. A click on a row opens the next level.

What we keep

  • We keep the click, the page pattern, the time, and the host your visitor came from. Nothing else.

  • Your users get a pseudonymous id and a session id. We set no cookie in your page.

  • We never store an IP address.

  • One call erases one user. Each click goes after 12 months.

The notice of the waitlist

  • The purpose. We use your address for the news of Octometer. We send no other mail.
